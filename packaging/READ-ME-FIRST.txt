Twinfold - how to open it the first time
=========================================

Twinfold isn't notarized by Apple yet (that costs $99/year and I'm one person),
so the first time you open it, macOS shows a warning like
"Apple could not verify Twinfold is free of malware". That's expected.

1. Drag Twinfold.app into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click Twinfold. When the warning appears, click Done (not Move to Bin).
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the Twinfold message. Confirm with your password.
4. From now on it opens normally.

Prefer the Terminal? This does the same thing:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Twinfold.app

The code is all open: https://github.com/Himanshu-Sangshetti/twinfold
You can build it yourself with `swift build` if you'd rather.

What Twinfold does to your files
--------------------------------
Nothing is deleted, moved or renamed. Identical files are compared byte for
byte, then made to share one copy of their data on disk (an APFS clone).
Each file still opens, edits and deletes on its own, exactly as before.
Every save shows up in History, where one click undoes it.

Questions or problems: https://github.com/Himanshu-Sangshetti/twinfold/issues
